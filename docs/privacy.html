Fergie Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

About Fergie

Fergie is a private Discord community assistant operated by fergielicious.

Pinterest Data

Fergie's Pinterest integration accesses Pins and Boards belonging to the Pinterest account that explicitly authorizes the integration.

This information is used to select fashion-related Pins from an account-owned board for periodic sharing in a designated private Discord channel.

How Pinterest Data Is Used

Pinterest data accessed by this integration is used only to provide Fergie's Pinterest-powered community feature. The integration is not offered to general Pinterest users.

Data Sharing

Fergie does not sell Pinterest data or provide Pinterest data to advertisers or data brokers.

Authorization

Pinterest access is authorized by the connected Pinterest account owner and may be revoked by removing the application's authorization.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy may be submitted through the project's public GitHub repository.
